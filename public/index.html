Our Mission

We Exist to
Close the Distance

Bridging The Gap was founded on the belief that poverty and inequity persist not because people don't care — but because the infrastructure to connect care with need is broken. We fix that.

Volunteer Opportunities

Your Time Changes Lives

We have programs for every schedule, skill set, and capacity. No experience required — just heart.

🥗

Food Distributions

Join our weekly food drives. We need packers, sorters, and drivers. Saturdays 8AM–12PM.

📖

Youth Tutoring

Volunteer as a tutor for K-12 students in math, reading, and STEM. Virtual or in-person options available.

🏠

Home Repair & Rehab

Help restore homes for elderly and disabled community members. Skills in trades a bonus but not required.

💼

Career Coaching

Mentor job seekers through resume reviews, mock interviews, and career guidance sessions.
